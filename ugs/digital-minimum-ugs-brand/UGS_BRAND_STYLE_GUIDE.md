# UGS Brand Style Guide for "Цифрові суперсили"

## 1. Context
This version aligns the service with **Ukrainian Global School** and uses the school mascots **Yuna** and **Yudji** as the main visual anchors.

Grounding sources:
- Ukrainian Global School website: https://globalschool.in.ua/
- UGS mascots provided by the project owner.

## 2. Core visual direction
The interface should feel:
- bright;
- optimistic;
- global;
- active;
- friendly for children 7–10.

Avoid heavy academic or corporate styling.

## 3. Main brand colors
Use these as the primary palette:
- UGS blue: `#0F59CF`
- Deep blue: `#0A398D`
- Sky blue: `#70D0FF`
- Lime green: `#B9DF16`
- Soft lime: `#F2F9D9`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Support colors for category differentiation are allowed, but the overall impression must remain **blue + lime + white**.

## 4. Brand motifs
Preferred recurring motifs:
- globe / world;
- stars and sparkles;
- rounded shapes;
- motion and discovery;
- soft orbit rings;
- backpack / school cues.

Use motifs as subtle decoration, not as visual clutter.

## 5. Mascot use
### Yuna
Best for:
- welcome;
- thinking;
- encouragement;
- confidence.

### Yudji
Best for:
- pointing to actions;
- movement;
- beginning a task;
- playful energy.

Rules:
- mascots are decorative helpers, not required for every screen;
- do not overload lessons with large mascot art;
- use them mainly on home/entry/empty/celebration states;
- keep mascot images on transparent backgrounds.

## 6. Child-facing copy and brand
Child-facing text should stay simple.
Allowed:
- “Разом з Юною та Юджі”
- “Створено у співпраці з Ukrainian Global School”

Avoid on child screens:
- long institutional descriptions;
- mission/value statements in adult language;
- technical explanations of the partnership.

## 7. Accessibility notes
Because blue is dominant:
- use deep blue for text, not mid-blue;
- maintain strong contrast on white backgrounds;
- lime should be used mostly as an accent, not as text on white;
- focus ring stays blue and visible.
